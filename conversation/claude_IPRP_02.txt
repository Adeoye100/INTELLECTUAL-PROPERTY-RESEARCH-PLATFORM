EUIPO does have an official API, not just third-party scrapers. dev.euipo.europa.eu is EUIPO's own developer portal, with a "Trademark search 1.1.0" product built for exactly this use case.

On cost: every page in their onboarding flow — account signup, app registration, API subscription — describes an approval gate, never a payment gate. No pricing tier, no paywall, no "contact sales" anywhere in their docs. That's the normal shape of an EU public-agency API: funded by the EU budget, free to registered users, rate-limited rather than metered by cost. I can't hand you a line that says "$0" in writing, because they don't publish one — but the complete absence of pricing language across their whole getting-started flow is itself the signal.

What "getting it" actually takes:

    Register an EUIPO account
    Register an "app" → get a Client ID + Secret
    Subscribe to the Trademark Search product, accept their Conditions of Use
    Wait for approval — sandbox: up to a week. Production: longer, unspecified
